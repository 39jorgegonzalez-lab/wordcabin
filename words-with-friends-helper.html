What is WordCabin?

WordCabin is a simple word finder that helps you turn scrambled letters into real words. It is built to be quick enough for games, clear enough for students, and calm enough for anyone who wants a friendly place to solve letters.

FAQ

Can I use a wildcard?

Yes. Use a question mark, like ?, to represent any letter.

Is this only for games?

No. It can also help with homework, vocabulary practice, spelling activities, and creative writing prompts.

Does WordCabin save my searches?

No. This launch version runs in your browser and does not require accounts or saved user data.
