# WordCabin Master Roadmap

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Active Development

---

# Vision

Build WordCabin into the most trusted, useful, and profitable word-generation and word-solving platform on the web.

The project is intended to become:

* A best-in-class word solver
* A trusted educational resource
* A scalable SEO platform
* A sustainable online business
* A long-term digital asset

Every milestone should improve one or more of these objectives.
