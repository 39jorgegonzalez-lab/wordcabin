# WordCabin 90-day growth operations

Start Day 1 after production closure. No traffic/revenue forecast or arbitrary page-volume target.

## Days 1–30: establish evidence

- Record a 28-day Search Console baseline: clicks, impressions, CTR, position, queries, landing pages and devices. Use direct owner exports if the paid connector is unavailable; do not purchase a subscription just to run a report.
- Verify production GA4 receipt and record page views, tool_engaged by tool_name, allowed daily events and returning-user trends. Never collect query letters/guesses/answers. Keep Enhanced Measurement and advertising features off.
- Weekly 30-minute review: largest useful query/page opportunities, failing routes, engagement changes, and one prioritized improvement. Compare equal settled periods; flag tiny samples and consent-related measurement limitations.
- Prioritize the three existing tools. Investigate high-impression/low-CTR or relevant near-page-one queries before revising title/copy; preserve canonical URLs and search intent.

## Days 31–60: improve demonstrated usefulness

- Publish only original, useful explanations addressing observed questions. Avoid mass keyword variants and dated indexed pages.
- Add contextual links among related existing guides/tools. Compare landing-page engagement before/after each bounded change.
- Identify legitimate puzzle/education communities and relevant resource publishers. Prepare useful submissions/outreach for owner approval; no unsolicited messages, paid links or automated spam.
- Resolve the daily editorial/publication policy before promoting a promise of new daily puzzles. Archive replay remains valid but is not new daily inventory.
- If display ads are selected, complete consent/CMP, disclosures, identifiers and Preview tests before activation. Approval alone does not mean ready to serve.

## Days 61–90: scale evidence

- Expand only topics/tools with demonstrated demand and useful engagement. Reassess weak pages rather than adding volume.
- Compare returning users and daily completion trends; do not equate denied-mode signals with exact visitors.
- If ads are active, review actual revenue, viewability and layout/performance impact; remove harmful placements. Without ads, report not applicable, not zero-income failure.
- End with a short decision list: continue, revise, stop. Keep routine maintenance monthly and growth reporting weekly.

## Measurement contract

GSC is the source for organic queries/impressions/clicks; GA4 is the source for consent-aware site engagement. Tool engagement is once per valid-result tool instance and only forwarded with consent. Daily completion is the original solve, not a replay. No new event vocabulary is required. Benchmarks come from measured baseline, not invented forecasts.
