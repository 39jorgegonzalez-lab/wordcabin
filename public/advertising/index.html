Advertising standards

Revenue without tricking the reader.

Effective September 15, 2026

Clear labels

Paid placements will be labeled as advertising or sponsorship and will not be disguised as solver results, navigation, or editorial recommendations.

Product independence

Payment does not determine which words appear in the dictionary or how solver results are ranked.

User experience

Advertising must not block the primary solver controls, imitate system warnings, trigger unexpected downloads, or make the site difficult to use on a phone.

Future partnerships

If WordCabin introduces affiliate links or sponsored content, the commercial relationship will be disclosed near that content.
