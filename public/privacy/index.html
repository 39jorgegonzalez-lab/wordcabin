Privacy

Plain-language privacy information.

Effective September 21, 2026

Word searches

The word solver runs in your browser. WordCabin does not require an account to use the solver.

Daily challenge progress

Daily Word Challenge completions and streaks are stored locally in your browser on this device. This feature does not create an account or synchronize progress with a server or another device. Clearing browser storage clears this local progress.

Traffic measurement

WordCabin may use Google Analytics to understand aggregate site usage. Analytics is optional and starts only after you choose Allow analytics. You can decline and still use every solver and challenge. WordCabin does not send solver queries, entered letters, filters, challenge guesses, or answers as analytics event data.

If you allow analytics, Google may use analytics cookies and receive ordinary technical and web-request information needed to provide Analytics, under Google’s privacy policy. Advertising remains separately controlled; allowing analytics does not enable advertising or ad personalization.

Use Analytics preferences at the bottom of any page to change your choice, including withdrawing consent. The versioned analytics preference is stored locally in your browser, separately from challenge progress, without personal information. Clearing browser data resets the preference. If browser storage is unavailable, your choice applies only to the current page. Withdrawing consent stops future analytics collection from the site; it does not erase information already sent.

Advertising

WordCabin may display clearly labeled advertising in the future. Advertising providers may process device or browsing information under their own policies. Advertising code remains disabled until WordCabin configures an approved provider.

Your choices

You may use browser privacy controls, content blockers, or leave the site. WordCabin does not sell information submitted to the word solver.

Changes

This page will be updated if WordCabin adds accounts, email collection, personalized advertising, or another feature that materially changes data handling.
